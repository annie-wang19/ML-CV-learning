# ML-CV-learning

[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

A learning journal for mathematics, Python, machine learning, computer vision, and English.

I am a third-year undergraduate preparing for future master's-level study. This repository documents my learning in machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision, alongside my progress in academic English and IELTS preparation. My IELTS target is an overall band score of 7.5, with a tentative test date around February 2027.

I mainly study on weekends. I use ChatGPT to help plan learning tasks, explain difficult concepts, review code, and improve my English. My goal is to understand, implement, test, and explain what I learn while maintaining good performance in my university courses.

## Learning goals

- Rebuild the mathematical foundations needed for ML and CV alongside Python programming. Prior coursework does not mean these foundations are secure.
- Study selected introductory material from Hung-yi Lee's machine-learning course.
- Learn geometric computer vision through Daniel Cremers's Multiple View Geometry course.
- Develop systematic AI-assisted programming habits and independently verify results.
- Practise Python through short daily exercises, selected LeetCode problems, and mathematical programming tasks, with a noon reminder and regular reviews.
- Improve English through regular reading, listening, writing, speaking, and IELTS-specific practice.

## Proposed roadmap

| Study weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–8 | Mathematical foundations, Python, NumPy, and orientation |
| 9–12 | Regression, classification, and model evaluation |
| 13–18 | PyTorch, neural networks, and a small image-classification project |
| 19–26 | Camera geometry, matching, and a two-view geometry demonstration |
| 27–28 | Reproducibility, review, and an English presentation |

Mathematics and English practice run throughout. Study weeks may be repeated or paused around university exams. The proposed eight-hour weekend includes two protected maths hours. Weekday practice provisionally adds 20 minutes each for Python and English. Weekend Python exercises count within the existing coding time. The technical roadmap extends beyond the IELTS target date; during the final four to six weeks before IELTS, I will allocate more weekend time to English and extend the technical schedule. The time budget will be revised after assessing my availability and current English level.

## Progress records

Each study week will include a small implementation or experiment, a short learning log, an unresolved question, and an English summary. I will record AI assistance and how I verified results.

Planned portfolio outputs are an image-classification experiment and a two-view geometry demonstration. These are learning goals, not completed projects.
